## Objective and findings

- Solana: [Jupiter Predict](https://jup.ag/prediction) brings markets sourced from Polymarket and Kalshi to Solana. [Jupiter Forecast](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/prediction/forecast) is its native offering for 15-minute BTC up-or-down rounds. The [Prediction API](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/prediction) documents market data and order creation with the user's wallet signature and states that the API blocks US and South Korean IP addresses.
- BNB Smart Chain: [Predict.fun](https://docs.predict.fun/) operates markets for crypto, sports, politics, and other events. [Binance Wallet](https://www.binance.com/en-IN/support/announcement/detail/4989a793bf58434980967848a0f16d77) offers access to Predict.fun through the Markets > Prediction tab of the Binance app in supported regions. [Opinion](https://docs.opinion.trade/developer-guide/opinion-clob-python-sdk/overview) is another BNB Chain venue with an official CLOB SDK.
- Regional restrictions and SignalVerse's right to route orders still need venue-specific verification. The [Predict.fun terms](https://docs.predict.fun/terms-of-service) list France and Iran as restricted. No SignalVerse integration, private account availability, order execution, or coverage/liquidity test was performed.
