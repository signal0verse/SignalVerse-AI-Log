## Code-path findings

1. The engine's entry side is driven by five indicator votes (EMA trend, MACD, RSI, StochRSI, Bollinger), with absolute score at least four and highest-scoring timeframe selected. Ties prefer the higher timeframe. Macro/risk gates can block. Structure levels and CPR are context, but default Real entry SL is 1.25 ATR and TP1 is 2R; independent S/R, OB or FVG validation does not anchor these levels. `confidence` is score magnitude divided by five, not a calibrated success probability.
2. `alignedCount` counts every non-neutral timeframe trend, even those opposite to the selected side, and is described as “aligned” in the reviewer prompt. This remains a misleading feature. In the absence of any qualifying timeframe, the engine logs `raw_score=0` and the first input timeframe as `chosenInterval`; all 76 current `WAIT` rows have that shape (`1h`), which is a fallback artifact, not evidence that 1-hour analysis scored zero or was the best timeframe.
3. Real entry needs an AI `CONFIRM`; `CAUTION`, `REJECT` and unavailable fail closed. Demo review is informational. The prompt forbids changing side, entry, SL or TP and gives compressed engine evidence, so the Supervisor cannot independently construct a structural trade or proactively manage an open position. During reanalysis it is called only for a valid same-side proposal. The Real reanalysis caller still builds the default timeframe set rather than the position's user-selected list, allowing weekly input to reappear. Profit-side/break-even stops remain rejected by the direction validator; loss-side widening lacks an explicit no-widening monetary-risk rule. These are code-confirmed limitations, not proof that a particular alternative would be profitable.
4. The previous audit's entry-fee and terminal-funding defects were partly addressed by later deployed commits `e5ce65c` and `d38ce3f`: exact entry/close fee lifecycle validation and delayed signed funding reconciliation now exist. The current active SHA includes them. They must **not** be reported as still unmodified. The stale historical safety reproduction fails three former-defect assertions because its old expected behavior no longer matches the changed fee function. No new post-active-SHA closed Real trade exists here to verify their private-account effectiveness.
