Learn how to use SignalVerse Spot with this step-by-step tutorial on spot copy trading, staged crypto buying, and capital management.

This video walks you through the Spot interface, from creating a setup and understanding why analysis is waiting to reviewing purchase scenarios, order statuses, profit calculations, and withdrawal records.

In this tutorial, you will learn how to:
- Create a spot trading setup and allocate capital to each asset.
- Understand why analysis is waiting and what the status card colors mean.
- Review scenarios, purchase ladders, and order statuses.
- Distinguish free capital, reserved capital, and capital used for completed purchases.
- Understand realized profit, unrealized profit, compounding, and average profit realization time.
- Record profit withdrawals and check the remaining profit.

Creating a setup does not mean buying immediately. Preparing a scenario, placing an order, and completing a purchase are separate steps. Recording a withdrawal is for bookkeeping only—it does not transfer funds from an exchange.
